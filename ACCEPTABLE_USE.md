# VOXPrint Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to the models we host (compressed and fine-tuned versions) and to the official VOXPrint builds.

1. **Lawful use only.** Do not use the models or builds, or any output made with them, in a way that breaks any applicable law or regulation, or that infringes the rights of others.
2. **No malicious or harmful use.** Do not use the models or builds to harm, deceive or exploit any person, or for any other malicious purpose.
3. **Pass it on.** If you redistribute the models or derivatives of them, include this policy or equivalent restrictions, and require your recipients to follow it.

This policy applies **in addition to** the upstream licence of each model and does not replace it. Where the upstream licence imposes stricter terms, those terms apply. The VOXPrint source code remains licensed under Apache-2.0.

We may update this policy. The current version is published with each model.
