# Original harmless creative-writing prompts. Apache-2.0.
Write a morning scene in a bakery as the first tray of bread comes out of the oven.
Describe a quiet train ride past birch trees in late spring.
A librarian finds a pressed flower inside a returned book. Write the moment.
Two friends repair a bicycle in a courtyard and argue about which road to take.
A potter opens the kiln and finds one cup glazed the colour of river water.
Write a letter from a lighthouse keeper to a sibling about a storm that missed the coast.
A child names every star they can see from a balcony, and an adult listens.
Describe the sound of rain on a canvas awning above an empty cafe.
