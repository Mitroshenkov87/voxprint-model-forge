The river kept the last of the daylight along its near bank. A woman in a wool coat counted the barges and lost count on purpose. Somewhere a radio played a song neither of them would remember. He said the city looked smaller from the bridge, and she said that was the point of leaving it for an hour. They walked until the lamps took over from the sky. Bread from a late bakery steamed through paper. Neither of them was in a hurry, and the story did not need them to be.
A second paragraph stays with ordinary objects: a chipped blue cup, a key on a string, a notebook that starts in the middle because the first pages were used for shopping lists. The writing is concrete so the matrix sees normal tokens, punctuation, and the shape of a sentence that ends when it should.
